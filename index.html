Sazan Coast is committed to responsible development, transparency, and meaningful engagement throughout the Environmental and Social Impact Assessment process. This platform provides stakeholders with clear and accessible information about the project, the assessment process, environmental and social considerations, consultation activities, and opportunities to provide comments, questions, concerns, or grievances.

The website supports applicable Albanian requirements for environmental information disclosure and public consultation, while reflecting IFC Performance Standards and Good International Industry Practice on stakeholder engagement, accessibility, transparency, and accountability. It provides a centralized public platform where stakeholders can follow the assessment and consultation process and access relevant project information throughout its development.
