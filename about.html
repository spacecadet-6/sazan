Story About the
Name ‘Sazan Coast’

Sazan Coast is a coastal project on the Zvërnec Peninsula, on Albania’s mainland. The peninsula is a distinctive stretch of coastline in the Vlora region, known for its natural landscapes, Mediterranean setting, and relationship with the Ionian Sea. Its position between the mainland and the open water creates a unique coastal environment, where forests, wetlands, beaches, and the sea come together.

The name Sazan Coast reflects this geographical setting and the character of the coastline–and an appreciation for the natural beauty that defines this region. From the peninsula, views extend across the water towards Sazan Island, creating a strong visual relationship between the two landscapes. It is this sense of place, the coastline, the peninsula, and its connection to the wider Vlora region, that gives the project its name. Sazan Coast is rooted in the Zvërnec Peninsula itself. It is a distinct mainland development with its own identity, vision, architecture, and approach to the landscape, shaped by the character of the peninsula and its connection to the wider coastal region.

Sazan Coast Masterplan is a comprehensive, nature-integrated coastal development spanning approximately 284 hectares of land along the Zvërnec Peninsula in southern Albania.

The vision is to create a destination where rchitecture, landscape, and the natural environment exist in harmony, establishing a new benchmark for ustainable coastal development while delivering long-term environmental, social, and economic value.

The masterplan is envisioned to be realized over approximately three to five years, with each phase carefully considered to ensure the destination evolves in harmony with its natural setting environmental commitments, and long-term vision.
